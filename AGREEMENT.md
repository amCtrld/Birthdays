# A Gentle Agreement

BDQueue is shared in good faith.

You are free to use, copy, modify, and distribute this project.
That freedom is granted with one expectation:

Treat it like a human thing.

## We ask that you

- Respect the philosophy of simplicity
- Avoid turning this into a growth or engagement machine
- Credit the origin when reasonable
- Keep contributions kind and intentional

## We know

This agreement is not enforceable.
It exists to set tone, not rules.

If you choose to ignore it, that is your right.
If you choose to honor it, you are our kind of builder.
